Strange Flesh v1.5.1 adds a multilingual edition, expanded controller support,
and a portable Windows executable.

- **17 languages**, including Brazilian Portuguese, Irish, Italian, German,
  French, Polish, Russian, both Chinese variants, Japanese, Korean, both Spanish
  variants, European Portuguese, Turkish and Ukrainian.
- **Language selection in Settings**, with broader translation coverage for
  story captions, enemy names, the boss announcement and credit headings.
- **Alagard-style accented text** for European languages and offline Noto
  fallbacks for Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Greek and Cyrillic characters.
- **Xbox, PlayStation and Switch Pro controller support**, family button labels,
  analog movement, configurable deadzones and remapping.
- **Music and SFX slider bars**, instant mute/volume changes, and restored
  original single-line title prompt and music behavior.
- **Portable Windows x64 .exe**, persistent desktop saves/settings, and a
  one-time importer for the original media ZIP.

Download the `.exe` and choose your existing **Strange-Flesh-Media-v1.4.zip** on
first launch. The media pack is distributed separately; see the
[project README](https://github.com/Tedenfe/Strange-Flesh#readme).

Download **CHANGELOG.txt** below for the complete list. The 15 additional
translations are AI-assisted drafts awaiting native-speaker review. Physical
controller playtesting is still pending; the Windows executable is unsigned.
